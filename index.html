Afterword

A site kept deliberately narrow.

This space is not built to explain everything. It holds a mood, a face, a measure of restraint, and enough room for the edges to stay visible.

The point is not volume. It is signal. A small number of details, shaped carefully, can say more than a page trying to account for itself.

Manifesto

Not more. Sharper.

The web does not need another page trying to imitate a product deck, a portfolio template, or a polite grid of achievements.

It can still be personal. It can still feel edited. It can still allow atmosphere to do part of the speaking.

This page is built around reduction, not absence. The cuts are the point. The empty space is part of the sentence.
