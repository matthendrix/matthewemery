Manifesto

Useful software over unnecessary theatre.

I like software that is clear, capable, and honest about what it does, which should not be a radical position.

That applies to products, infrastructure, internal tooling, and the small public surfaces around them. A lot of things get better when they stop pretending to be more profound than they are.

I am usually more interested in reliability, legibility, and fit for purpose than novelty for its own sake. The bug tracker already provides enough novelty.
